# Soundtrack direction (A11)

A11 is implemented as a provenance-first soundtrack layer: original scoring may be shipped alongside new recordings/renderings of public-domain Revolutionary and Napoleonic-period repertoire.

The initial station seed contains two valid Vorbis tracks registered through the normal HOI4 music asset/playlist system:

- **Council of Europe** — original composition and original synthetic rendering.
- **La Marseillaise Rendering** — a new instrumental rendering of the 1792 public-domain composition. No historic or commercial recording is copied.

Every future track must record composition provenance and recording provenance separately. A public-domain composition does **not** make a modern recording public domain, because apparently copyright law enjoys side quests.
